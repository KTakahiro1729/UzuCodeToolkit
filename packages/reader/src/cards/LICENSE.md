# Playing cards

SVG playing cards by Adrian Kennard (https://www.me.uk/cards/), released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain, no attribution required).

Changes: the court cards (J, Q, K) show a large rank and pip in place of the
figures, to keep the deck small; metadata was removed. The joker (Kennard's
`2J`) is `joker.svg`.
