# Timed phases and a facilitator are opt-in parts

The owner wants parts a work chooses to use, not defaults pushed on every work. A timed text phase (`timedPhase`) and a game-select facilitator or per-part memo exist for plans that ask for them ("discuss for 10 minutes", "the GM picks the games"). A work that names none gets today's behaviour: no timer, and everyone may pick. A read-through followed by a game needs no part: consecutive phases with the same band label already merge in the footer.
