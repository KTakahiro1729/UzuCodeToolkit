# Each picture is generated as a part from the start

An image-generating agent makes every picture of the order sheet in a generation of its own, at the size the sheet gives. It does not generate a whole scene and cut the parts out: a cut part has fewer pixels than its box needs and comes out blurred. Transparency that is imperfect (a leftover edge, a rectangle) is accepted; resolution lost to cutting is not. The order sheet therefore says so in its introduction and rules, and its per-scene lists are marked as where parts sit, not pictures to make (they used to say "draw the finished scene in one image", which invited the whole-scene picture the agent then baked into the background). Characters are standing pictures of their own, never part of the background. Cutting a part out of a whole picture by code stays out of the toolkit.
