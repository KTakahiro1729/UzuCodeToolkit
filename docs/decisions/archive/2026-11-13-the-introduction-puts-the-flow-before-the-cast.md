# The introduction puts the flow before the cast

Order on the introduction screen: story text, the game's flow, then the cast. On a landscape screen the story text and the flow stack in one column, with the cast in the other. The flow is what players need to know before the part list, and in landscape the text and the flow are both short reading blocks that fit one column. Replaces the earlier order (text, cast, flow) and the landscape layout where the flow ran across both columns under them. Code: `CastView.svelte`; `docs/design.md`, Introduction with the cast.
