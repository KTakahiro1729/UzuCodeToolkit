# Overflow found by the numbered-screen check: wide cards stay, the layout gives way

Cards stay 4em wide (index a quarter of the width); a hand that does not fit overlaps its cards (the last stays whole) or sits under the name instead of beside it, never shrinks the cards. The sheet's scrim reaching past its section is intended and marked `data-overflow-ok`. The handout tabs and the band's phase pill lose padding (and the pill 2px of text) to fit a portrait phone.
