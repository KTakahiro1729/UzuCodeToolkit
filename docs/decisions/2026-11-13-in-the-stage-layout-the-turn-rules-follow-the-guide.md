# In the stage layout the turn rules follow the guide

On the stage layout (see the-official-read-through-layout-is-a-second-screen.md) the next button shows only on your turn, inside the frame at its lower right, and no one sees a greyed one. Nameplate ② tells who reads next: when you read, it holds the next mark and the next reader's name; when another reads, it holds the next mark and the next line, on one line cut with an ellipsis, in your colour if you read next. Your turn is also your colour on the frame and on nameplate ①, with the label for your turn.

This replaces, for the stage layout only, "both players see the button, greyed for the other" (show-what-to-do-on-your-turn.md) and "the next reader is not shown" (do-not-show-who-reads-next.md); the chat and the games keep them. Why: it is what the guide specifies, so players meet the same screen across works, and the stage shows one line at a time, so its nameplates and colour have to carry the turn that the chat's pile of lines carried. The cost is that the main button is no longer in the footer on this screen, while it stays there in other phases; the guide is meant to grow to the other screens, which should bring them to the same place.

Not taken: keeping the greyed button in the frame (a button no one can press, against the guide) or the footer (the guide has no footer).
