# Wide screens have columns, and the handouts stay beside the screen

A screen 40em wide and 480px tall (in the screen's own em) takes 2 columns, 60em wide takes 3; phones keep one column in both orientations. Once the handout phase is over, the handouts stay in view on a wide screen, in the column(s) left of the current screen, instead of opening as a sheet from the footer; the current screen takes the last column. The footer's actions centre under the current screen. Screens before the handouts keep their single reading column. Details in `docs/design.md`, Layout.
