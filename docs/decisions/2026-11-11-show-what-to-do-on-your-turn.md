# Show what to do on your turn

Right under the newest line, both players see the advance button: filled for the reader, greyed for the other. It says `hint.readThenTap` until your first line is read, then shows the next mark alone; on the last line it says `button.end` instead. Tapping elsewhere does nothing. Each line shows the speaker's name; in the top band your role's icon has a double circle, and band text is bold.
