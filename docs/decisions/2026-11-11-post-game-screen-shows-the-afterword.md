# Post-game screen shows the afterword

After the last line, the title and end mark hold for a moment, then they fade out and the post-game screen fades in after them (200 ms each, one at a time, instant with reduced motion): the centred greeting `review.heading` and under it an area labelled `review.afterword` holding the author's afterword (`afterword` in `script.json`, the author's words verbatim, CC BY 4.0 with the script). No tap needed.
