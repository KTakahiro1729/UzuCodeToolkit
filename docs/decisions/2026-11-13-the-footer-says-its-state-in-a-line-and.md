# The footer says its state in a line, and red is only for danger (owner, 2026-11-13)

Tried six footer designs side by side and had Haiku read the 43 screens of InterstellarBG without help. What it misread most was not the wording but whether a button could be pressed or had been pressed: it could not tell the pressed next button from one still to press, it could not tell why two grey buttons were grey, and it could not tell that a greyed next button waits for the settings. So the upper row carries one sentence of state (`status.*`: waiting for others, the other's turn, fill the settings, who has to tap), and the buttons keep their labels, size and place. A pressed toggle (次へ) is outlined with a dark 2px edge and a tick instead of red (`--color-undo` is removed). Red is kept for danger (`--color-danger`, `Button variant="danger"`), and a danger button is never the rightmost of the main row, where the button to press now sits. Not taken: shorter labels with symbols (they do not say what happens), a second line inside the buttons (it makes the footer taller in landscape), a separate cancel button (it breaks the toggle decision). The wording of the upper row's "HO" is not decided. Measured on 13 catalogue screens of the work (HO and one game): 15.4 s with the first version, 8.0 s after shorter quiet windows (50 ms between steps, 80 ms before the shots), no settle between steps that wait for their own element, and a resize that waits for the frame size and two frames. Cost grows linearly with the screens (each screen is played from the previous one, about 0.35 s of work per screen, plus 0.6-0.9 s where a reload is needed); `overflow.js` is O(elements x depth), 6 ms a screen. Shots differ from the old script's only in a running clock's digits. The harness holds one shared game, so screens cannot be played in parallel against one harness; going below this means several harnesses with `--only` groups.
